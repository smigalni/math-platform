# Mathematical review

Review the task and its proposed solution independently; do not assume the author's answer is correct.

## Checklist
- Are definitions, assumptions, units and domains explicit and correct?
- Are algebraic transformations valid, including restrictions introduced by division, logarithms, roots and cancellation?
- Are signs, parentheses, exponents, indices and interval endpoints correct?
- Are limits, continuity statements, derivatives and asymptotes justified where relevant?
- Does the final answer address exactly what the question asks?
- Is the solution method appropriate for the student's level and the methods already taught?
- Do alternative valid methods or edge cases change the answer?

## Verification
Use symbolic computation or numerical checks when useful and available, but explain why the check is relevant. Test boundary values and counterexamples where appropriate. A computer algebra result does not replace a pedagogical explanation.

## Report
Classify findings as:
- **Error:** mathematically incorrect.
- **Ambiguity:** multiple interpretations or missing conditions.
- **Improvement:** correct but clearer reasoning is possible.

Give the exact location, reason, correction and verification evidence. Never report a check as independently performed if it was not.
