# Pedagogical review

1. Identify the target grade/course and relevant curriculum competence aims.
2. Check prerequisites against the actual sequence of the book, not assumed prior knowledge.
3. Check that explanations introduce concepts before relying on them in exercises.
4. Evaluate progression from accessible entry tasks to transfer and challenge.
5. Check whether examples model the intended method without giving away the exercise.
6. Check language, notation, context and instructions for ambiguity and unnecessary reading load.
7. Look for opportunities for reasoning, representation, communication, modelling and digital tools where they genuinely serve the learning goal.
8. Check that differentiation is meaningful and that advanced material does not obscure the core path.
9. Distinguish curriculum coverage from a chapter's contribution to a broader competence aim.

Report findings with evidence and suggested revisions. Do not force every competence aim into every chapter or change the agreed scope without the owner's decision.
